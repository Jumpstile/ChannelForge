# ChannelForge Manifesto

We do not guess.

We verify.

We do not blindly trust.

We establish confidence.

We do not silently change data.

We explain every decision.

We do not destroy.

We backup, verify, and recover.

We do not fear change.

We detect it, understand it, and heal from it.

## The Five Pillars

1. Truth
2. Trust
3. Recoverability
4. Determinism
5. Self-Healing

## Golden Rule

The user should never have to fix something ChannelForge can prove how to fix.
